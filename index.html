01Featured case study

Requisition Management System

Designed an Excel/VBA system to centralize requisitions, supporting documents, order status, and operational reporting across complex purchasing workflows.

  • ✓ Automated document loading, linking, and duplicate prevention
  • ✓ Simplified active-order tracking and fulfillment workflows
  • ✓ Added statistics for volume, status, and frequently ordered sets
ExcelVBAWorkflow designOperational analytics

Challenge: Paper-heavy records and separate folders made retrieval, follow-up, and status visibility time-consuming.

Approach: Mapped the end-to-end workflow, standardized data entry, and built automated controls around document handling and order completion.

Outcome: Estimated to save more than 300 staff hours annually, while reducing printing, stapling, filing, and physical storage needs.
