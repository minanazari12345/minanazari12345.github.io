The challenge

Maintenance dates, contracts, service reports, and vendor contacts can become difficult to monitor when stored separately. Missed visibility can lead to delayed follow-up and reactive work.

My solution

I designed a structured dashboard with status logic, document access, reporting controls, and communication tools.

TrackEquipment, serial numbers, sites, vendors, and next PM dates.
AlertIdentify overdue and upcoming maintenance at a glance.
ActGenerate reports and prepare vendor follow-up emails.

Key capabilities

  • Filters for location, vendor, and maintenance status
  • Links to service reports and contracts
  • Upcoming preventive-maintenance reporting
  • Vendor contact information and email-ready due reports
  • Color-coded status views designed for quick scanning

Portfolio descriptions use generalized workflow information only; operational records and internal documents remain private.
