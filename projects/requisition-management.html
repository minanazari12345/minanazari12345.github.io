Requisition Management & Analytics System

An Excel/VBA system that replaced fragmented, paper-heavy tracking with a centralized workflow for requisitions, documents, fulfillment, and operational reporting.

ExcelVBAWorkflow designOperational analytics
2,157requisition records centralized
91%fulfillment rate—1,956 records

The challenge

Requisitions and supporting files were spread across paper records and separate folders. Finding a document, confirming its status, and completing follow-up required repeated searching, printing, stapling, and filing.

My solution

I mapped the workflow and created one system for loading, linking, tracking, fulfilling, reopening, and reporting on requisitions.

01 · CentralizeStandardized requisition records and relative document links.
02 · ControlAdded duplicate prevention, status logic, and guided workflows.
03 · AnalyzeBuilt statistics for volume, fulfillment, and frequently ordered sets.

PS-DATA workflow

I created a semi-automated data workflow: download a Provincial Power BI export, save it with the receiving-slip records, then sync it into PS-DATA. The process cleans and matches purchasing records and supplies structured data for RMS reporting. I implemented field mappings, validation rules, and purchasing business logic, and troubleshoot mismatches and duplicate records.

This is an Excel/VBA-based data pipeline with a manual export step. It does not require a direct Power BI connection or a scheduled cloud service.

Analytics and estimated impact

The reporting layer covers 1,913 unique VMIDs, 8,179 order lines, 894 distinct requisitions, 1,809 purchase orders, and 24,435 units ordered since April 2025. The system also tracks 1,045 set-order records, including 190 instrument and 855 implant-and-screw records.

The 600+ annual staff-hours figure is an estimate based on approximately two Equipment Clerk hours and 30 coordinator minutes saved per working day, using roughly 240 working days per year. These are workflow estimates, not time-study measurements. Requisition and fulfillment counts are as of September 7, 2026.

What I delivered

  • Active and fulfilled order views with search and filtering
  • Automated document loading, linking, renaming, and retrieval
  • Controls for completing and reopening orders
  • Statistics covering categories, volumes, and frequently ordered items
  • SOPs, flowcharts, examples, and video-based training resources

Confidentiality: No patient information, internal documents, supplier details, or live organizational data are published in this portfolio.
